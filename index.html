Introduction

Why workflow matters more than presets.

In wedding photography, speed without system creates chaos. One missing backup, one inconsistent skin tone, one wrong export: the client notices. This book is Scout Studio's 7-step post-production SOP, built for high-volume wedding and event work.

Out of seven stages, six run in the background through scripts and AI models. Your active time is concentrated into a single, high-efficiency Quality Control block.

Goal: cut active post-production from 12–15 hours per wedding down to 2–3 focused hours, with the same artistic standard, so 4–6 peak-season weddings a month never means burnout.

Good editing is not about making photos beautiful. It's about making 800 photos feel like one beautiful album.

01

Chapter 01 · Backup First, Edit Later

File Management

Move every card to a safe, structured home, automatically and in the background, before any editing begins.

At a glance

Mode
Background
Duration
~30 min
Human effort
Passive · 0 min
Main tool
Automated card transfer
Goal
Secure double backup (RAW + JPEG)

Tools used

  • 64gb SD Card Camera (SanDisk/Kingston)
  • SD Card Reader (Ugreen)
  • 1TB SSD SATA (Faxiang & Ugreen cover)
  • Standard Folder Template
  • RAW + JPEG  (large/ medium) dual-format capture

Why this step

Transfer cards as soon as you are back from the shoot, or on the commute, and let the ~30 minute copy run while you do other work. We shoot RAW and JPEG together: the JPEG is an instant second backup and lets you share unedited proofs if a client asks.

Dual-format strategy

  • Shoot RAW and JPEG simultaneously.
  • JPEG works as an instant second backup.
  • It also allows quick unedited proof previews on request.

SOP checklist

  1. Create Client Folder in local storage/ Hard disk: Even name_ClientName_Venue_DD_MM_YYYY (e.g. Wedding Aini Amirul KL 20_5_26)
  2. Inside: Create 01_RAW, 02_Jpeg, 03_Pre Edit, 04_Finalized/Delivery
  3. Copy Card 1 & Card 2 into 01_RAW & 02_Jpeg. Do not format cards yet.
  4. Dual format check: confirm both RAW and JPEG both completely transferred.
  5. Verify file count matches RAW & Jpeg (e.g. 2500 files). Check for corrupt files.
  6. File rename: Select All the photo files, choose 1st file photo and rename it into first alphabet name of bride & groom. Eg. A&A (1). Numbering will be automatic. Make sure RAW name is matching with Jpeg. 
  7. Tick checklist in SOP Checklist
  8. Only then format SD cards. Keep SSD-BACKUP until final delivery confirmed by client.

Pro tips

  • Make sure date and time (in camera shoot) of photos is correct to prevent chaotic during editing process.
  • Never edit directly from SD card. Always copy first.
  • Start the transfer straight after the shoot or on the drive home. It runs unattended, so no active time is lost.

Avoid

  • Formatting cards before double-checking file count.
  • Mixing two weddings in lightroom folder without separate folders.
  • Using different naming styles per photographer — confuses editor.

Chapter 02 · From Thousands of Frames to Keepers

Photo Selection (Culling) by AI

Let AI evaluate focus, blinks, exposure and composition so you only confirm the story.

At a glance

Mode
AI automation
Duration
~10 min
Human effort
Passive · 2 min
Main tool
Narrative Select (AI)
Goal
Filter blinks, out-of-focus shots and duplicates

Tools used

  • Narrative Select (Recommended)
  • Lighroom AI Culling Assistant (For photo <200)

Why this step

Automated evaluation cuts culling from hours to about 10 minutes, with only ~2 minutes of human attention. AI filters the technical fails; you confirm the story, the emotion and the must-have moments.

Tool assessment

  • Lightroom AI: slow for more than 200 heavy RAW files.
  • Aftershoot: subscription cost, and it can drop valid shots.
  • Narrative Select: recommended. Fast and accurate.

SOP checklist

  1. Open Narrative Select > Create New Project > Import File 01_RAW folder
  2. Let AI run. Wait for Scenes grouping. Do not interrupt or closed your computer.
  3. Review AI Picks: Tick all the best scene & above average scenes. Quickly scan average scene for missed moments. ×
  4. Target: 35-50% keep rate. Thousands of frames down to roughly 35-50% keepers.

Pro tips

  • Use Scenes view: Choose max 3 best per scene, not 10 same pose.
  • Flag Bride & Groom solo - be strict, keep only 2 best per pose.
  • Select closed eyes scene to select any intentionally closed eyes scene. 

Avoid

  • Keeping 1500 photos 'just in case' — slows entire workflow.
  • Deleting emotional blurry shot that has story value. If menangis but slight motion blur, keep.
  • Culling large file inside Lightroom - slower and no face check.

Chapter 03 · 80% of Color Done in 10 Minutes

Pre-editing by AI

Batch-apply a wedding AI profile calibrated for event lighting to set exposure, contrast, shadows and base white balance.

At a glance

Mode
AI automation
Duration
10–30 min
Human effort
Passive · 2 min
Main tool
Narrative Edit / AI profile
Goal
Base exposure and colour harmonization

Tools used

  • Narrative Edit (AI Profile Edit Theme need to select)
  • Lightroom Classic

Why this step

The AI evaluates each frame individually across 1,000–3,000 photos, so nobody starts from zero. It runs in the cloud or background in about 10–30 minutes ("ship and edit"), which removes the first round of manual slider tweaking. Warm Western profiles get neutralized later in QC.

SOP checklist

  1. Click Ship & Edit > Choose current selected photos
  2. Off button to export photo directly into Lightroom. It is sometimes cause error.
  3. Select location of file where you want to save your pre-edited photos. Recommended to create file name Pre-Edited inside your project file.
  4. Click Edit button with AI profile. You are free to explore any other Ai profile but we used Bright & Light profile.
  5. Press button ship & edit and let AI pre-edit your files within 5-10 min.

Pro tips

  • Always run your AI culling & Edit in background to safe your editing focus hours.
  • Always safe your pre-edit photo file inside the local storage to prevent of loss data.

Avoid

  • Avoid shut down or closed Narrative Edit during it edit in background to prevent restart the process.
  • Avoid to export and edit directly into lightroom, if any error happened need to restart the process.

Chapter 04 · Structure That Editors Can Follow Blindfolded

Photo Import into Lightroom

Import the pre-edited album files into Lightroom Classic (or Cloud) and let smart previews render in the background.

At a glance

Mode
Background
Duration
~15 min
Human effort
Passive · 1 min
Main tool
Lightroom Classic
Goal
Build the smart preview cache

Tools used

  • Lightroom Classic 13+
  • Smart Previews

Why this step

Smart previews take about 15-20 minutes to render in the background, so the QC pass runs with zero lag or load delays. Collections and filters are ready, so the editor knows exactly what to do. 

SOP checklist

  1. Open Lightroom Classic 
  2. Select Pre-edit folder in your local storage and drop inside lightroom
  3. Click button Build Smart Previews: Library > Previews > Build Smart Previews

Pro tips

  • One wedding = One catalog. Never mix.
  • Smart Previews are compulsory - editor can edit without SSD.

Avoid

  • Building 1:1 previews for 800 photos - wastes 2 hours and 40GB.
  • Not creating collections - editor scrolls 800 photos randomly.
  • Importing without lens correction - bengkok pelamin looks unprofessional.

Chapter 05 · Where Good Becomes Scout Standard

Quality Control (QC / Finalization)

Fine-tune the AI edits in one focused 2–3 hour block so the gallery meets the Scout Studio standard.

At a glance

Mode
Human focus
Duration
2–3 hours
Human effort
Active · 100%
Main tool
Lightroom Classic (manual)
Goal
Fine-tune local tones, framing and scene sync

Tools used

  • Lightroom Classic
  • QC Checklist (PDF)
  • Reference Monitor / Calibrated Screen

Why this step

This is the single manual step. AI gets the batch most of the way there; QC adds the human eye: neutral skin tones, softened contrast and clean framing. Everything else runs in the background, so protect this block.

Critical syncing rule

  • Never bulk-sync settings across the entire event at once. Lighting changes constantly through the venue.
  • Group and sync by scene and composition. This prevents mass exposure errors.

SOP checklist

  1. Work in Collection Order: 01_Nikah -> 02_Outdoor -> 03_Pelamin -> 04_Family -> 05_Details -> 06_Reception
  2. Adjust Exposure, shadow & highlight if needed
  3. White balance neutralization: soften overly warm Western AI profiles toward neutral, natural tones.
  4. Sync Skin Tone: Match Bride skin across all photos. Use HSL Orange -10 Sat, +5 Lum.
  5. Crop & Straighten: Check photo need straighten or not especially for grouping & portrait. Crop photo if needed to remove any distraction and ensure to leave breathing space.
  6. Sync by scene and composition only (e.g. Akad Nikah stage together, reception tables separately). Never bulk-sync the whole event. Repeat same process until the last photos.

Pro tips

  • Edit in order of event flow - you will see story, not just photos.
  • Take 10 min break every 45 min - eyes reset, you catch color drift.
  • If stuck between two crops, pick wider - client can crop tighter, cannot add back.

Avoid

  • Bulk-syncing one setting across the entire event batch.
  • Over-editing skin until it looks not neutral.
  • Inconsistent exposure: Outdoor bright, then suddenly dark - client notices.
  • Skipping family group QC - uncle's face dark because backlight, must lift shadows.

Chapter 06 · Three Sizes, Zero Mistakes

Photo Export

Export full-resolution JPEGs to a dedicated folder, separate from the RAW archive, which stays untouched.

At a glance

Mode
Automated
Duration
15–30 min
Human effort
Passive · 1 min
Main tool
Lightroom batch engine
Goal
Generate deliverable high-res JPEGs

Tools used

  • Lightroom Classic Export Presets (Scout v4)
  • SSD / Google Drive
  • Folder 04_Exports

Why this step

Batch export takes about 15–30 minutes in the background. A wrong export means rework, so presets decide size, quality and naming. Original RAW files are never modified.

SOP checklist

  1. Export: Select All photos > JPEG, sRGB, High Quality 100, No Resize
  2. Select your Finalized/Export folder then export. Wait for 15-30 min, do not closed lightroom or shut down your computer.
  3. File Size Check: FULL ~15-25MB each and count total export number make sure same as in lightroom
  4. Update Sheet: Export Done + File Count + Total GB. Example: 847 photos / 12.3GB FULL

Pro tips

  • Always export to Export/Finalized folder, never Desktop - easier for uploader to find.
  • Keep original file number in name for traceability - easier when client wanted to edit again'

Avoid

  • Exporting in AdobeRGB - colors look dull on phone.
  • Resizing FULL version - client cannot print large album.
  • Forgetting to remove watermark for full delivery - client angry.

Chapter 07 · Final Mile That Client Remembers

Photo Upload for Delivery

Deliver galleries through Google Photos for high-quality interactive viewing, with a download deadline and an optional USB upsell.

At a glance

Mode
Delivery
Duration
~15 min
Human effort
Passive · 2 min
Main tool
Google Photos gallery
Goal
Client delivery and USB upsell

Tools used

  • Google Photos (shared album)
  • Folder 04_Exports
  • WhatsApp Business + Email Template
  • Custom USB Drive (optional add-on)

Why this step

Delivery is client experience, not just upload. Google Photos gives the couple a fast, interactive gallery. A download expiry date keeps cloud storage costs under control, and a custom USB drive is offered as a premium backup add-on.

SOP checklist

  1. Create a Google Photos album, rename a same name as local folder. 
  2. Upload the full gallery. Keep the laptop awake and wait for 15-30 min.
  3. Check: open the shared link in an incognito window. Confirm photo count, order and quality.
  4. Set the download expiry date and state it clearly in the delivery message.
  5. Send WhatsApp: gallery link, expiry date and a 1-2 line personal thank-you note.
  6. Offer the USB add-on: a custom drive with the full-resolution files as a premium backup.
  7. Update the Master Sheet: Delivered, expiry date, USB status. Keep SSD-BACKUP archived for 90 days.

Pro tips

  • Upload the Sneak Peek within 2-3 days of the wedding. Client excitement is highest then.
  • Enforce the expiry date every time. It keeps storage costs predictable.
  • Mention the USB option in the delivery message, not after the link has expired.

Avoid

  • Sending the gallery link with no expiry date.
  • Uploading from a laptop that goes to sleep, so the upload fails near the end.
  • Forgetting to offer the USB add-on while the client is still excited.
